Privacy Policy

The Lost Voice: Language Quest
Published by Lingo Quests (Daniel Badillo, individual developer)
Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app The Lost Voice: Language Quest (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed and published by Daniel Badillo under the developer name Lingo Quests ("I", "me", "my").

1. Information collected

The App does not collect, store, share or send any personal data. Specifically:

  • There are no accounts or logins.
  • There are no ads.
  • There are no analytics, tracking or crash reporting tools.
  • The App does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Your learning and game progress is saved only on your phone. It is never sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or by clearing its data in Android Settings (Apps → The Lost Voice → Storage → Clear data).

3. In-app purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, they are processed entirely by Google Play. I do not receive or store your payment details or other personal information. Google handles purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing

Because the App collects no personal data, there is nothing to share or sell.

5. Data security

Your progress stays on your device, protected by Android's built-in app storage protections. No data is sent over the internet by the App.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Future features

If a future version of the App adds a feature that collects data, such as voice practice using the microphone, I will update this Privacy Policy before that version is released, and the App will ask for your permission before using any such feature.

8. Changes to this policy

Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
